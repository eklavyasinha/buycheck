# BuyCheck — 3-Minute Demo Video Script & Plan

## Video Overview
- **Product Name:** BuyCheck
- **Tagline:** Shop smarter. Pay with confidence.
- **Target Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds
- **Key Themes:** AI transparency, dark pattern detection, true cost calculation, and deterministic conditional PayPal checkout.

---

## Storyboard & Script

### 0:00 – 0:30 | The Problem & Introduction
**Visual:** Open on the BuyCheck landing page. Smooth scroll showing the clean dark luxury aesthetic inspired by hole.money.
**Narrator:**
> "Have you ever found a product online advertised for ₹4,999, only to discover at checkout that hidden taxes, fragile handling fees, and a sneaky 7-day recurring subscription added thousands more to your bill?
>
> Today, e-commerce checkout is filled with dark patterns, unannounced restocking deductions, and hidden trial conversions.
>
> Welcome to **BuyCheck**—an AI shopping intelligence and payment protection platform that reveals the real cost and risks of any purchase before you pay."

---

### 0:30 – 1:15 | The Interactive Analysis Experience
**Visual:** Click "Instant Guest Demo" or "Analyze a Purchase". Click the sample scenario "ANC Headphones with Free Trial Trap" or paste a product URL.
**Narrator:**
> "Let's run an audit. BuyCheck supports three input methods: public product URLs with SSRF protection, screenshot uploads with Gemini Vision OCR, or pasted terms.
>
> We'll click our sample scenario: ANC Wireless Headphones.
>
> In real time, BuyCheck’s multi-stage pipeline reads the merchant data, extracts advertised prices, audits hidden fees, and inspects subscription clauses using Google's Gemini API."

---

### 1:15 – 1:55 | The Verified Purchase Report
**Visual:** The Report page renders with the risk badge, True Cost breakdown bar chart, and evidence quotes.
**Narrator:**
> "Here is our verified report.
>
> First, our True Cost Calculator proves that the advertised ₹4,999 price is actually ₹6,047.82 confirmed upfront due to an 18% GST and a ₹149 packaging surcharge.
>
> Second, our Dark Pattern Detector uncovered an automatic monthly subscription of ₹999 hiding inside a '7-day VIP pass', complete with the verbatim evidence quote from the terms.
>
> Third, the Policy audit flags a 15% restocking fee on opened boxes, and an informational PayPal Purchase Protection evaluation explains digital versus physical claim conditions.
>
> You can even ask our contextual AI assistant questions grounded strictly in this report."

---

### 1:55 – 2:30 | Deterministic Conditional PayPal Checkout
**Visual:** Click "Proceed to Protected Checkout". Show the evaluation modal displaying rule checks.
**Narrator:**
> "Now for our most important feature: **Deterministic Conditional Checkout**.
>
> The AI never authorizes money on its own. Instead, our backend rule engine compares the merchant terms against your personal rules.
>
> Here, the purchase is initially flagged because it violates our rule: 'Block automatic recurring subscriptions'.
>
> Once the user reviews and acknowledges the specific risks, BuyCheck creates a secured PayPal Sandbox order, locking the authorized amount.
>
> We authorize through PayPal Sandbox, and upon server-side verification, the payment is captured and permanently logged."

---

### 2:30 – 2:45 | Conclusion & Call to Action
**Visual:** Show the transaction ledger and return to the main dashboard showing real analytics.
**Narrator:**
> "BuyCheck turns blind trust into evidence-backed confidence. Fullstack, production-ready, powered by Gemini and PayPal Sandbox.
>
> Thank you!"
